02 / MISSION ARCHITECTURE

One platform. Repeated missions.

One system.
Six mission phases.

LELP separates permanent lunar infrastructure from the payload that returns. The platform lands once, then repeats the collect–package–launch cycle.

10–25 kgplatform mass
Solar + Li-ionrechargeable power
5–1200 gMVP sample range
01◎

Earth launch

A commercial launcher places the compact transfer vehicle into Earth orbit.

02◫

Lunar landing

The lander deploys LELP, solar arrays, communications and the sampling system.

03⌁

Survey & collect

Cameras map local terrain while the robotic tool selects rock and regolith.

04⬡

Verify & package

Material is identified, weighed, sealed in a capsule and logged for traceability.

05↗

Electromagnetic launch

Supercapacitors discharge through the launcher while the platform stays on the Moon.

06◇

Guidance & recovery

A lightweight stage corrects the trajectory toward orbit or a future return architecture.
