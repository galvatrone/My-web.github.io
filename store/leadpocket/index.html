FAQ

Questions before you start.

Is LeadPocket a full CRM?

No. LeadPocket is intentionally focused on incoming opportunities, ownership and follow-up. It gives small teams the parts of a sales workflow they actually need without becoming a large CRM.

Who is LeadPocket designed for?

LeadPocket is designed for small service businesses and teams where every incoming enquiry can become real revenue.

Can leads come from my website?

Yes. Leads can be created manually or captured through a webhook so website enquiries can enter your LeadPocket workflow.

Can I see overdue follow-ups?

Yes. LeadPocket keeps the next action visible and highlights opportunities that need attention.

Can I start with a small team?

Yes. LeadPocket is intentionally designed around a lightweight workflow without a long CRM setup process.
