Before the first nudge

A few useful answers.

Is this debt collection software?

No. InvoiceNudge is an invoice reminder and payment-status workspace. It does not provide debt collection or guarantee payment.

Does InvoiceNudge process the payment?

This workflow tracks invoice status and follow-up. You mark an invoice paid when payment arrives through your existing payment arrangement.

Can I bring my existing invoices?

You can add invoice records individually or import them from a CSV. Keep the client, amount and due date together.

What if a customer needs more time?

Pause or snooze follow-up and record a promise-to-pay note, so the next action reflects the conversation.

Does this demo send real reminders?

No. This page uses illustrative data only. Sending reminders in the application requires a configured email sender.
