FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is ReviewLoop? +

ReviewLoop is a focused tool for small service businesses that want a clearer workflow around asking customers for reviews and keeping track of follow-up.

Is ReviewLoop a full CRM? +

No. ReviewLoop is intentionally focused on the customer review workflow rather than trying to replace a full customer relationship management system.

Who is it designed for? +

It is designed around small service teams such as agencies, contractors, workshops, consultants and local service businesses.

What happens after a job is completed? +

ReviewLoop keeps the customer and the review request visible so the team has a clear follow-up path instead of relying on someone remembering later.

Why not just ask customers manually? +

You can. The problem ReviewLoop focuses on is consistency: making the review request part of a visible workflow so good opportunities for feedback are less likely to be forgotten.
