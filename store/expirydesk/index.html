Before you start

A few useful answers.

What can I track?

Dated business records such as contracts, licences, insurance or certificates. You supply and maintain the relevant expiry dates.

Does ExpiryDesk renew a contract for me?

No. You complete the renewal outside the tracker, then update the record. The example button only demonstrates a change in the tracked date.

Can I attach a reference document?

The current application supports a validated document URL. It does not claim built-in binary document storage.

Does the time slider affect real records?

No. It is a local demonstration of urgency changes using illustrative data.
