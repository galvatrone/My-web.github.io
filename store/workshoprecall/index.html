Before you start

A few useful answers.

Does this send messages from the demo?

No. The page only illustrates the lifecycle of a service record. Production reminder delivery requires a configured email sender in the application.

Is the reminder a confirmed appointment?

No. A reminder starts a conversation about a future visit. It is not a booking or a guarantee that the customer will return.

Can customers opt out?

The application includes customer opt-out controls. Keep communication preferences current before sending a reminder.

Can I export my workshop records?

The application includes CSV export and customer data export/deletion workflows.
