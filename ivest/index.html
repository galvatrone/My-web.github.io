Position

Urban air logistics, built to last

We focus on the operational layer: the rules, safety framework, and coordination needed to make urban air delivery real. The technology exists — the missing piece is the system that makes it trusted and scalable.

Vision

Vector Technologies is not building drones. We are building the next layer of urban logistics.

Who we are

Vector Technologies is an early-stage team working at the intersection of aviation, logistics, and urban policy.

We believe cities need a new logistics layer that reduces congestion and unlocks faster, cleaner delivery. Our work blends aerospace engineering, AI operations, and regulatory alignment so urban air logistics can move from pilots to real public infrastructure.

We are based in Cyprus with a European outlook. The first step is a compliant pilot environment; the long-term goal is a multi-city network that becomes a standard part of urban logistics.

The problem

Urban logistics is reaching its physical limits.

Traffic congestion continues to slow deliveries, while last-mile costs rise faster than revenue. Cities cannot scale road infrastructure indefinitely, which turns logistics into a bottleneck for urban growth, commerce, and sustainability.

The result is predictable: delivery delays, increased cost per order, and an ever-growing strain on existing urban systems. Ground-only logistics is no longer enough.

Our solution

Vector VTOL delivery platform, built for regulated deployment.

Vector provides an urban VTOL platform that enables air logistics without requiring customers to change their behavior. Our focus is on creating a repeatable, legally compliant delivery layer that partners can rely on.

We design for fleet-scale operations, not one-off demos. That means the platform is structured around reliability, safety, and measurable performance — while keeping proprietary implementation details private.

Why now

Urban air logistics is finally feasible — technically and legally.

On-demand delivery continues to expand across food, pharmacy, and instant commerce. At the same time, the EU has accelerated green mobility and smart city initiatives. Most importantly, U-space regulation now exists, creating a legal framework for routine urban air operations that were previously impossible.

Battery and AI maturity make autonomous deployment realistic, and the regulatory environment is aligned. The window to build the next logistics layer is open.

Market & business model

Drone-as-a-Service with a compliance-first rollout.

Vector operates on a Drone-as-a-Service model: we own and operate the fleet while partners pay per delivery or per zone. This keeps adoption simple and avoids requiring customer behavior change.

Cyprus is the pilot market: compact geography, dense urban areas, and tourism-driven demand. The longer-term vision is a European network shifting a meaningful share of urban logistics to air across multiple cities.

Regulation & U-space

We are designed to fly legally first, scale second.

Vector aligns with EASA regulatory pathways, structured risk assessment (SORA), and U-space integration requirements such as Remote ID and geo-awareness. Our approach prioritizes safety, compliance, and trust — because without that, nothing scales.

Roadmap

A clear progression from pilots to scale.

Year 1 focuses on the VTOL MVP and pilot programs. Years 2–3 expand fleet operations in Cyprus. Years 4–10 are dedicated to European expansion and multi-city deployment.
