About Me

Mihail Charilaou (Михаил Харилау; also written Mikhail or Michael Charilaou) is a software, AI and systems engineering student at the University of Nicosia, Cyprus, and a software developer at Oneix Ltd.

I build SaaS products, applied AI, secure data-transfer systems, and robotics and aerospace concepts in Python, C++, Rust and JavaScript. I like projects where the whole path can be seen and measured: from raw data to a result on screen, from a message sent to a message acknowledged.

My main interests are offline AI architectures with memory and adaptive behaviour, cybersecurity and secure systems, autonomous multi-agent systems such as drone swarms, and aerospace concepts. I work between software and hardware: Arduino and ESP32 boards, sensors, Linux and networking, and neural networks written by hand as well as with libraries.

I speak Russian and Greek natively and English at a working level. Outside code I run a student robotics team and keep notes on what worked and what did not, some of which I publish on the blog.
